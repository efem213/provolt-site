About ProVolt Engineering

15 Years of Excellence in Electrical Infrastructure

Our Foundation

Founded by Mehmet Efecan Akpullukcu, ProVolt Engineering was established to bridge the gap between complex electrical design and field-ready project management. With over 15 years of professional experience specializing in construction and infrastructure, our firm brings a high-level technical perspective to every project. Based in Long Beach, California, we are deeply integrated into the state's municipal and educational procurement systems, including Los Angeles World Airports (LAWA) and the City of San Francisco.

Strategic Project Management & Consulting

At ProVolt, we differentiate ourselves by focusing on Consulting and Project Management. We serve as the technical bridge between owners and contractors, providing expert oversight, QA/QC, and construction administration. Our mission is to ensure that infrastructure projects are delivered with technical precision, adhering to strict safety standards and SBE compliance requirements.

Please note: ProVolt Engineering focuses exclusively on project management and engineering consulting. We do not perform electrical installations as a prime contractor at this time.

Core Leadership

Our Principal Engineer holds advanced degrees in Electrical Engineering, Cybersecurity, and an MBA in Project Management. This multidisciplinary background allows ProVolt to manage modern infrastructure projects that require both traditional power expertise and advanced digital security integration.
